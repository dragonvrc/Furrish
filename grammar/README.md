# Furrish Grammar (v0.1, MVP, EN)

- **Consonants:** m n p b t d k g f v s z h w y r (rr) l (rare) ch [t͡ʃ] j [d͡ʒ]
- **Vowels:** a e i o u (long by doubling: aa, ee, ...)
- **Syllable:** (C)V(N/R). Allowed clusters: mw, ny, rw, kw, gw.
- **Word order:** SVO.
- **Particles:** wa (TOP), ya (OBJ), na (of/possession), ko (at/in), ka (Q), no (negation).
- **Tense/aspect:** ta (past), nu (progressive), zo (future).
- **Copula:** ye (optional).
- **Negation:** no before verb.
- **Adjectives:** after the noun.
- **Plural:** -i or reduplication for many.
- **Diminutive:** -chu; **augmentative:** -RAH (stylistic).
- **Mood tags:** prr, grr, chrrp, eep, snrk, nom (optional).
